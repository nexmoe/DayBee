AISHELL-3 cohort embeddings
==========================

This directory contains speaker embeddings derived from the AISHELL-3
Mandarin speech corpus. The original recordings are not included.

Source
------
Dataset:    AISHELL-3
Identifier: OpenSLR SLR93
URL:        https://openslr.org/93/
Publisher:  Beijing Shell Shell Technology Co., Ltd.
External:   http://www.aishelltech.com/aishell_3

Citation
--------
Yao Shi, Hui Bu, Xin Xu, Shaoji Zhang, and Ming Li.
"AISHELL-3: A Multi-speaker Mandarin TTS Corpus and the Baselines."
https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.11567

What is bundled
---------------
cohort_eres2netv2.f32
  288 little-endian float32 embeddings, 192 dimensions each.
  Nine non-held-out speakers, 32 windows each.
cohort_meta.json
  For every window: source WAV name, sample offset, sample count,
  SHA-256 of the source WAV, and SHA-256 of the window PCM.

Speakers included
-----------------
SSB0193, SSB0407, SSB0686, SSB0702, SSB0711,
SSB0716, SSB0717, SSB0736, SSB0749

Speakers held out of this cohort
--------------------------------
SSB0434, SSB0544, SSB0693, SSB0700
No window from these four speakers is present in the embedding file.

Model used to compute the embeddings
------------------------------------
3dspeaker_speech_eres2netv2_sv_zh-cn_16k-common.onnx
